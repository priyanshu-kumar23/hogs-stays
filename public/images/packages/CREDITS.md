## Honest-labelling notes

- No freely licensed photographs of **Soyal village**, **Rumsu** or **Pandu Ropa** were found. Those days use a generic Kullu Valley, Hampta Valley or Naggar timber-architecture image, and the on-page captions say so. Replace them with HOGS' own photographs when available.
- The Sajla image shows the forest approach, not the waterfall.
- Attractions with no genuine photo (Soyal, Sajla Village, Rumsu, Jana Village, Pandu Ropa, chai stops, Himachali lunch) reuse the nearest honest image, and the caption says what the photo actually shows. A freely licensed photo of Himachali dham was found but only showed prasad rice at another temple, so it was not used.
